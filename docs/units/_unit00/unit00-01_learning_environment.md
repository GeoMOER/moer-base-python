## Course Meetings

The course combines self-paced learning with regular weekly course meetings.

Students are encouraged to work through the learning materials before attending the corresponding course session. During the meetings, concepts are discussed, questions are answered, and practical programming exercises are completed.

At the end of each unit, a short Knowledge Check is offered to help students assess their understanding of the material. These Knowledge Checks are intended as learning opportunities and self-assessment tools.

---

## Structure of the Learning Materials

The learning materials are organized into the following components:

### LM (Learning Material)

Provides theoretical background information, explanations, examples, and additional resources.

### EX (Exercises)

Practical programming exercises designed to reinforce the concepts introduced in the learning materials.

### KC (Knowledge Check)

Short self-assessment activities offered at the end of each unit to support continuous learning and identify topics that may require additional practice.
