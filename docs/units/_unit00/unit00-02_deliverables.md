The course distinguishes between **study requirements (Studienleistung)** and **examination requirements (Prüfungsleistung)**.

Depending on your study program, you may be required to complete only the study requirement or the examination requirement. Please consult your examination regulations if you are unsure which requirements apply to you.

---

## Study Requirements (Studienleistung)

To successfully complete the study requirement, students must:

1. Participate in at least **80% of the Knowledge Checks**.
2. Submit the final project by the announced deadline.

The Knowledge Checks are not graded and are intended to support continuous learning and self-assessment throughout the semester.

The final project serves as evidence that students can apply the concepts learned during the course to a practical programming task.

---

## Examination Requirements (Prüfungsleistung)

To successfully complete the examination requirement, students must:

1. Participate in at least **80% of the Knowledge Checks**.
2. Complete the final written examination.

The written examination assesses the theoretical and practical knowledge acquired during the course.
